## How Engagements Are Structured

**Assessment** - A fixed-scope engagement producing a current-state inventory, findings, risk register, and a sequenced roadmap with effort estimates. Typically one to three weeks.

**Proof of concept** - A narrow, production-representative build that proves a pattern and de-risks the larger investment before commitment.

**Implementation** - Phased delivery against the roadmap with defined milestones, acceptance criteria, and knowledge transfer built into each phase.

**Enablement** - Instructor-led training, labs, office hours, and operating-model guidance so your team can run and extend the solution without ongoing dependency.

**Advisory retainer** - Recurring architecture review, escalation support, and roadmap guidance at an agreed monthly commitment.

Every engagement includes documented architecture decisions, reusable assets, and a knowledge-transfer plan. The objective is a capable internal team, not a permanent dependency.
