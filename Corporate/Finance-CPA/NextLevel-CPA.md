An LLC and an S-corp aren’t mutually exclusive: the LLC can remain the legal entity while electing S-corp tax treatment. The LLC provides the liability structure; the election changes how business income is taxed. The transcript is in NextLevel-CPA.md.

When each option makes sense

Stay with the LLC’s current tax treatment if net profit is below that range, is unpredictable or temporary, or if payroll and added tax-return costs outweigh the potential savings.
Consider the S-corp election if net profit is consistently above that range and you expect to keep consulting for multiple years. You would need to pay yourself a reasonable salary through payroll; remaining profit may be distributed separately.

Treat that as a rough, pre-cost illustration, not a guaranteed net savings. Distributions generally remain subject to income tax, and payroll, tax-preparation, and other costs reduce the benefit.
