## Purpose

This document defines how I position my services, discuss rates, negotiate effectively, and maintain a value-based consulting practice focused on Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, Analytics Modernization, and Data Platform Transformation.

# Customer Discovery Process

## Step 1: Understand the Business Driver

Ask:

- Why is this project being undertaken?
- What business outcome is expected?
- Is there an executive mandate?
- Is there a licensing deadline?
- Is there a modernization initiative?
- What happens if the project fails?

Goal:

Move the conversation from technology to business value.

---

## Step 2: Understand the Risk

Ask:

- How many users are impacted?
- Which reports are mission critical?
- Are executives using these reports?
- How complex are the calculations?
- How much business logic exists today?
- What timeline constraints exist?

Goal:

Help the customer recognize business risk.

---

## Step 3: Define Success

Ask:

"What does success look like two years after the migration is complete?"

Possible answers:

- Tableau fully retired
- Reduced licensing costs
- Standardized governance
- Trusted reporting platform
- Faster delivery model
- Fabric adoption

Goal:

Define measurable outcomes.

---

# Migration Positioning Framework

For Tableau-to-Power BI engagements:

Do NOT say:

> I migrate Tableau reports.

Instead say:

> I help organizations successfully retire Tableau, modernize analytics, implement governance, establish scalable Power BI and Fabric foundations, and reduce migration risk.

The first sounds tactical.

The second sounds strategic.

Strategic work commands strategic rates.

---

# How to Discuss Rates

Never start with price.

Always start with:

1. Business Goals
2. Risks
3. Outcomes
4. Scope
5. Then Rates

---

## Recommended Language

> Before we discuss rates, let's confirm what success looks like and where I can provide the most value.

After discovery:

> Based on what you've described, this is less of a report conversion effort and more of a business and technology transformation.

Then:

> For migration architecture, modernization planning, Fabric strategy, governance, semantic modeling, and advisory services, I typically work in the $225 to $275 per hour range depending on scope and level of involvement.

Stop talking.

Do not justify.

Do not negotiate against yourself.

# Handling Rate Objections

Customer:

> Your rate is higher than expected.

Response:

> I understand. Can you help me understand what range you were expecting and what role you're looking for?

Listen first.

---

## If Customer Says $150/hr

Response:

> At that rate you're typically looking for implementation resources and report development assistance.
>
> My focus is migration leadership, architecture guidance, governance, Fabric strategy, and helping reduce the risk of a multi-year transformation effort.

---

# Never Lower Rate Immediately

Bad:

> Okay, I'll do $175.

Good:

> There may be flexibility depending on scope, duration, and commitment level.

Always trade rate for something:

- More hours
- Longer engagement
- Monthly retainer
- Defined commitment

Never reduce rate for free.
