# Statement of Work Templates

Every SOW states an objective, deliverables, commercial terms, client
responsibilities, and explicit exclusions. An SOW without exclusions is not complete.

## Assessment SOW

Objective:

Evaluate the current environment and develop a migration strategy.

Deliverables:

- Assessment report
- Executive presentation
- Roadmap
- Risk register

Commercial:

- Fixed fee with stated hour cap
- 50% at signature, 50% at delivery of the executive presentation

Client responsibilities:

- Named business owner and technical owner
- Environment access within 5 business days of signature
- Subject matter expert availability for scheduled interviews

Exclusions:

- Report or dashboard development
- Data platform changes
- Remediation of findings

---

## Architecture SOW

Objective:

Design the target-state architecture.

Deliverables:

- Governance framework
- Security model
- Semantic modeling standards
- Deployment standards

Commercial:

- Fixed fee with stated hour cap, or hourly at the Architecture & Migration Lead tier
- Change orders required for scope added after design approval

Client responsibilities:

- Decision-making authority available for design review sessions
- Timely approval of standards documents

Exclusions:

- Implementation and build
- Tenant or capacity administration
- Ongoing operational support

---

## Advisory Retainer SOW

Objective:

Provide ongoing strategic guidance.

Deliverables:

- Weekly office hours
- Architecture and design reviews
- Executive briefings
- Migration oversight

Commercial:

- Monthly retainer billed in advance
- Stated hours included per month, no rollover
- Overage billed at the applicable hourly tier
- 3-month minimum term, 30 days notice to terminate thereafter

Client responsibilities:

- Standing meeting cadence with consistent attendees
- Materials submitted in advance of review sessions

Exclusions:

- Hands-on development
- Production support and on-call coverage
- Project management of client staff

---

## Solution Delivery SOW

Objective:

Deliver a governed Microsoft 365 solution replacing an uncontrolled manual process.

Deliverables:

- Approved requirements and field mapping
- Solution design and build
- Workflow configuration
- Pilot and user acceptance testing support
- Administrator and end-user handoff documentation

Commercial:

- Hourly at the Standard Advisory tier, or fixed fee by approved scope
- Licensing or connector requirements discovered during validation are a change trigger

Client responsibilities:

- Site owner and tenant administrator access
- Confirmation of licensing and connector availability
- Named testers for user acceptance testing

Exclusions:

- Premium connector, Dataverse, or Power Pages licensing costs
- Integration with systems outside the approved scope
- Ongoing administration after handoff
