# Customer Script

> Before we discuss rates, I want to make sure we're aligned on what success looks like.
>
> From what we've discussed, this isn't simply a report migration project. This is a transformation effort focused on retiring Tableau, modernizing analytics, and ensuring users can successfully adopt Power BI and Fabric.
>
> My role is helping reduce risk, establish governance, design scalable architecture, and ensure the migration is successful.
>
> For those services I generally work in the $225 to $275 per hour range depending on scope and duration.

---

# Objection Handling

Customer:

> Your rate is higher than we expected.

Response:

> I understand. Can you help me understand what range you expected and the type of role you're looking for?

---

Customer:

> We only have budget for $150/hr.

Response:

> At that rate organizations are typically hiring implementation-focused resources. My focus is helping reduce the risk associated with strategic migration and modernization decisions.

---

# Negotiation Rules

Never lower rates immediately.

Trade for:

- More hours
- Multi-month contract
- Retainer agreement
- Larger scope

Never trade rate for nothing.
